Our Delicious Recipes

Find step-by-step instructions to prepare healthy and delicious meals.

Chicken Salad

Ingredients:

  • Grilled chicken breast
  • Mixed greens
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil dressing

Instructions:

  1. Chop the grilled chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Mix with greens, tomatoes, and dressing.
  3. Serve fresh and enjoy!

Quinoa Power Bowl

Ingredients:

  • Cooked quinoa
  • Avocado
  • Grilled vegetables
  • Black beans

Instructions:

  1. Cook quinoa according to package instructions.
  2. Dice avocado and grill vegetables.
  3. Mix everything together and serve.

Grilled Salmon

Ingredients:

  • Salmon fillet
  • Steamed broccoli
  • Brown rice
  • Lemon butter sauce

Instructions:

  1. Season salmon with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill for 5–6 minutes per side.
  3. Serve with steamed broccoli and brown rice.

Vegan Protein Bowl

Ingredients:

  • Tofu
  • Kale
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions:

  1. Roast sweet potatoes at 400°F for 25 minutes.
  2. Sauté tofu and kale with sesame seeds.
  3. Combine and serve warm.

Healthy Pasta

Ingredients:

  • Whole wheat pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Grilled chicken
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Boil pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Cook chicken and mix with tomato sauce.
  3. Top with parmesan cheese and serve.
