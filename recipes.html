Our Healthy Recipes

Find quick and easy recipes to fit your busy lifestyle.

Avocado Toast with Eggs

Calories: 320 kcal

Ingredients:

  • 1 slice whole-grain bread
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1 boiled egg

Instructions:

Mash avocado, spread on toast, and top with sliced boiled egg.

Grilled Chicken Salad

Calories: 450 kcal

Ingredients:

  • 1 grilled chicken breast
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • Cherry tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions:

Mix all ingredients, add dressing, and toss lightly.

Berry Smoothie

Calories: 250 kcal

Ingredients:

  • 1 banana
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries
  • 1 cup almond milk

Instructions:

Blend ingredients until smooth.

Grilled Salmon with Quinoa

Calories: 520 kcal

Ingredients:

  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 1/2 cup quinoa
  • 1 cup steamed broccoli
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions:

Season the salmon with salt, pepper, and olive oil. Grill for 4-5 minutes on each side. Cook quinoa as per package instructions and serve with steamed broccoli.
